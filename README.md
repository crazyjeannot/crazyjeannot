
### Hi!
I'm Jean Barré, a postdoctoral researcher in computational literary studies at the [Max Planck Institute for Empirical Aesthetics](https://www.aesthetics.mpg.de/) in Frankfurt, where I work in the ERC project [MELT – Macroevolution of European Literature](https://www.aesthetics.mpg.de/en/the-institute/people/oleg-sobchuk.html). I did my PhD at [ENS-PSL](https://www.ens.psl.eu/) & [Lattice](https://www.lattice.cnrs.fr/).
- I'm interested in literary history, cultural evolution, NLP, machine learning, stylometry and more...
- I try to operationalize concepts from literary theory (canonicity, genre, archetypes, literary change) and model them in large literary corpora, mostly French fiction (19th–20th c.).
- 🛠️ I maintain [Propp-fr](https://lattice-8094.github.io/propp/), an NLP pipeline for French novels, and the [Refined Gallica Corpus](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20233178) (11k French novels).
